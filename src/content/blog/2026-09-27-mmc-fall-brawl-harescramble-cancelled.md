title: MMC Fall Brawl Harescramble Cancelled
pubDate: 2026-09-27T12:45:36.880Z
description: |-
  It is with considerable regret that the MMC must announce the immediate cancellation of today's planned hare scramble event. This difficult decision has been made primarily due to the severe and ongoing conditions, which have rendered the racing trails unraceable and unsuitable for competition.

  The safety and well-being of all our participants, volunteers, and spectators remain our utmost priority. Proceeding with the event under the current hazardous conditions would pose unacceptable risks and compromise the integrity of the race. We understand this news will be deeply disappointing, and we sincerely apologize for any inconvenience or disruption this last-minute cancellation may cause. We appreciate your understanding in this matter and encourage you to monitor our official channels for future updates regarding potential rescheduling or other information.
