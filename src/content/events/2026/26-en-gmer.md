Welcome to the 2026 Three Springs Enduro!

This event is being brought to you by the Green Marble Enduro Riders and the Community of

Three Springs, Pa. Many local businesses, residents and landowners have spent many hours

organizing and preparing for this event. We hope you enjoy it.

Sign is up at the Fire Hall on Saturday from 4:00 PM until 6:00 PM & on Sunday from 7:00

AM until 9:00 AM. PLEASE BRING YOUR HELMET TO SIGN UP. \*\*\*\*\*\*You must sign

up by Sunday at 9AM even if you are on a late row\*\*\*\*sign up CLOSES at 9am

Sunday\*\*\*\*.

Key Time and any course changes will be posted at the Fire Hall by 7:00 AM on the day of the

event.

Event t-shirts and a limited supply of hoodies will be for sale at sign up.

Only one gas can is needed. There will be one (1) gas truck serving one GAS AVAILABLE at

ground mile 29 course mile 50.30. Look for the gas trailer near the fire house pavilion.

Some road crossings will be manned. Please pay careful attention to directions given by the

road crews/ and Fire Police.

Should you experience a medical or mechanical problem, please stay on the course and wait for

a course marshal, or sweep rider, for assistance. Do not ride the course backwards. Should you

find someone in need of assistance, note the mileage and report it to the check crew, sweep or

marshal.

Don't be THAT GUY! Obey local traffic laws, be respectful to the Community, residents, your

fellow competitors and their guests.

Please OBEY THE SPEED LIMIT on public roads. We have built in generous resets to get you

back on or head of time. The course will take you through residential areas on both blacktop

and gravel roads. Slow signs will be posted near Churches still having services. Please slow

down and keep noise to a minimum. After Section 6 PLEASE RIDE SLOWLY THROUGH

THE FARM. AGAIN, PLEASE be respectful of the residents.

The course will also take you along fields of hay, corn, buckwheat, soy and other crops.

Please, unless otherwise directed by course markers, STAY OUT OF THE CROPS!

ALL property used for this event is PRIVATE PROPERTY and is only available for your use

on the day of the event. Please do not return and ride the trails after the event has

concluded....doing so will jeopardize our future Enduros.

Parking for LARGE RVs is available at the community swimming pool. Additional parking

and camping is available in the 2 large fields at the entrance to the town on the RIGHT. Parking

and camping is on a first-come, first-serve basis. Please no generators after 10:00 pm. Open

fires are prohibited. Please do not park on the streets. The area directly adjacent to, and

directly behind, the Fire Hall is reserved for GMER members.

NO Go Pros or tear-offs.

For you, your family & guests, the community swimming pool is available for your use free of

charge on Saturday from 1:00 PM until 9:30 PM and on Sunday from 1:00 PM until 6:00 PM.

If you like you can grab a quick shower there before your dive home.

Alcohol is prohibited at the Fire House and the Swimming Pool.

The Fire Company will be hosting an all you can eat Spaghetti Dinner Saturday Night

from 6pm until 8:00+.

The Lions Club will be offering breakfast on Sunday morning and food after the race along

with the Spring Creek Diner a few blocks away. The Pizza Star restaurant will be open

Saturday evening and after the race for dine in or take out..

If it's hot PLEASE HYDRATE. To help:

The Friends OF The Three Springs Community Pool will have complementary snacks

and drinks at Gas Available. Thank the ladies for their support.

The property owners of Section 5 of the day will have complimentary drinks available.

Thank the ladies for the use of their property. It's these folks that make our event

possible.

There will NOT be a course split this year. All riders ride all sections.

Green Marble Enduro Riders and the Community of Three Springs hope you enjoy your stay

and have a safe, fun race.
